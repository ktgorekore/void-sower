## 🎨 Developer Page Visual Branding

To configure the developer page in Google Play Console (**Developer page** section):

1. **Developer Icon** ($512 \times 512\text{ PNG}$):
   - Circular geometric dreadnought emblem with solar gold chevrons on deep obsidian black.
2. **Developer Header Banner** ($4096 \times 2304\text{ PNG}$):
   - Wide-angle cinematic rendering of the Kilwa Nebula Basin with orbital plasma lance discharge.
3. **Short Description** (Max 140 chars):
   `Pioneering high-performance Afrofuturist games bridging ancient mathematical heritage with modern kinetic gameplay.`

---

## 💳 Merchant Account Configuration

To enable the `$0.99` In-App Purchase (`void_sower_pro_lifetime`):

1. Navigate to **Google Play Console** > **Setup** > **Payments profile**.
2. Link or create a Google Payments Merchant Profile.
3. Complete tax identity information (W-8BEN / W-9 depending on business entity jurisdiction).
4. Configure settlement bank account for monthly payouts.
5. Verify customer support email and billing descriptor displayed on credit card statements: `GOOGLE*VOID SOWER PRO`.
