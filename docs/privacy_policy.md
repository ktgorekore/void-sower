# Privacy Policy — Void Sower: Bao Orbital Batteries

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 13, 2026

## 1. Overview
Void Sower ("the Application") is an offline-capable mobile arcade and puzzle game published by the Void Sower Authors. We respect your personal privacy. The game operates under a strict data minimization model: game progression, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device.

## 2. Information Collected
- **Local Game State:** High scores, unlocked sectors, and preferences are kept within device private application storage.
- **Analytics & Crash Reports:** Aggregated, anonymous telemetry (e.g. wave completion, crash traces) may be logged to improve game balance and fix technical issues.
- **No Personal Identity:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical locations.

## 3. Advertising & In-App Purchases
- **Rewarded Advertisements:** If presented, ads adhere to Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent policies. Users can decline tracking consent without penalty.
- **In-App Purchases:** Transactions for Pro Lifetime license are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not store payment or card details.

## 4. Right to Be Forgotten (GDPR / CCPA)
You may permanently delete all local game telemetry and stored progress at any time by navigating to **Fleet Telemetry > Erase Guest Data (GDPR)** in the application.

## 5. Contact
For inquiries regarding this privacy policy, please contact: `voidsower-dev@googlegroups.com`.
