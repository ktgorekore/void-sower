1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

Void Sower Authors ("we", "us", or "our") operate the mobile game Void Sower: Kinetic Mancala ("the Game"). We believe player privacy is a non-negotiable right. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you download, install, or play the Game on Android or other supported platforms.

Key Summary: Void Sower is designed from the ground up as an offline-first tactical game. We do not sell your personal data, we do not require account creation to play, and you can enjoy 100% of the game offline without providing personal information.

2. Information We Collect

A. Local On-Device Game Data

All primary gameplay state is stored exclusively on your device's local sandboxed application storage:

  • Pilot Profile: Call sign, avatar selection, and combat telemetry records.
  • Campaign Progress: Sector liberation stars, high scores, wave records, and Kilwa Basin unlocks.
  • Fleet Hangar Inventory: Unlocked dreadnought hulls (MK-I Bastion, MK-II Monsoon Vanguard, MK-III Singularity Sovereign), weapon module configurations, and capacitor upgrades.
  • Preferences: Audio volume, SoundPool sound effects, tactile haptic settings, and camera shake intensity.

B. Anonymous Technical Diagnostics & Crash Logs

To maintain the stability of our high-performance C++17 EnTT simulation engine across diverse Android hardware:

  • We may collect anonymous crash diagnostics and performance telemetry (e.g., via Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Data includes device model, CPU architecture, GPU renderer string, operating system version, and symbolicated C++ native stack traces.
  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII), biometric data, or GPS coordinates are ever collected or processed.

C. In-App Purchases & Monetization

Void Sower does not employ predatory pay-to-win mechanisms or recurring subscriptions. If you choose to unlock the optional lifetime Admiral upgrade:

  • Transactions are processed securely by the **Google Play Billing System**. We never see, receive, or store your credit card details or billing address.
  • Your purchase entitlement status is tied directly to your Google Play account.

3. Consent Management (GDPR, CCPA & IAB TCF v2.2)

For players residing in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, Switzerland, or California:

  • We adhere strictly to the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • Non-essential diagnostic telemetry is subject to user opt-in/opt-out preferences in compliance with the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families)

Void Sower complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Developer Program Policies:

  • The game is rated ESRB Everyone / PEGI 3.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under 16 in applicable European jurisdictions).
  • All gameplay is accessible without personal data entry.

5. Data Deletion & Player Rights

Because your gameplay data is stored locally on your device, you have complete and total control over its lifecycle:

  • In-Game Reset: Open the in-game Settings modal and tap Reset Pilot Progress to wipe all locally cached save files and preferences.
  • OS-Level Clearing: Navigate to your Android device's Settings > Apps > Void Sower > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstallation: We author explicit `data_extraction_rules.xml` and configure `android:allowBackup="false"` to guarantee that uninstalling the application instantly purges all local state without silent cloud restoration.

6. Security Practices

Any outbound diagnostic network requests utilize industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Our native C++ codebase compiles with modern hardening flags (`-fstack-protector-strong`, `-D_FORTIFY_SOURCE=2`) and undergoes continuous static analysis to prevent memory leaks and buffer overruns.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted to this URL with an updated revision date.
