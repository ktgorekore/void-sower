Data Deletion & User Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

We give you complete autonomy over your personal and gameplay data:

  • Instant In-Game Purge:
    • In Oware 2048: Tap Settings > Privacy & Legal > Reset All Game Data.
    • In Void Sower: Tap Settings > Privacy & Legal > Reset All Game Data.
    This immediately and irreversibly wipes all local player profiles, high scores, campaign progression, and unlocked assets from device sandboxed storage.
  • Cloud Identity Deletion: If you linked a Google Sign-In account or Play Games Services profile and wish to permanently delete all server-synced cloud backups and player identifiers, email us with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
