Welcome to the Void Sower Legal Portal

Void Sower: Kinetic Mancala is an Afrofuturist orbital dreadnought tactical strategy game developed by Void Sower Authors. Our game bridges ancient East African mathematical board game traditions (Bao la Kiswahili) with modern high-performance arcade simulation.

We are committed to ethical game design, zero predatory mechanics, player privacy, and complete transparency. This portal serves as the authoritative source for our compliance policies, terms of service, and privacy disclosures.

⚡ Privacy-First & Offline-Ready: Void Sower requires zero mandatory user registration. All game progress, star ratings, and pilot telemetry remain strictly local to your device's sandboxed storage. We never sell or broker your personal data.

Compliance & Safety Standards

Regulation / Body Status & Implementation
GDPR (European Union) Compliant. Zero behavioral tracking, local on-device storage, one-tap instant local data reset.
CCPA / CPRA (California) Compliant. No sale or sharing of consumer personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
COPPA & Google Families Compliant. Rated ESRB Everyone / PEGI 3. No targeted ads or behavioral profiling of children.
Android 15 Page Alignment Compliant. Native C++17 ELF shared libraries aligned to 16 KB memory boundaries (`-Wl,-z,max-page-size=16384`).
